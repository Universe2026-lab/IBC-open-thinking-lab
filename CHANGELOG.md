- Display titles adopted: **Exploration 01 — Intelligent Beings: Competition and Coordination** and **Exploration 02 — The Multidimensional Evolution of Intelligence**.

## v1.1 — Mathematical scope and epistemic-status revision (2026-10-21)

- Added explicit definition, metaphor, hypothesis, candidate-model and open-placeholder labels.
- Replaced property/entity subset notation and additive conceptual expressions in the current framework.
- Separated unknown categories (`U_cat`), unknown dimensions (`U_dim`), rules (`K`) and empirical model error (`e`).
- Made category labels nonexclusive and spectrum language explicitly metaphorical.
- Replaced unrestricted future-function and threat-monotonicity expressions with scoped hypotheses.
- Distinguished workflows and possible mechanisms from established causal effects.
- Replaced the collective-output sum with a proposed matched-performance comparison; no measured synergy is claimed.
- Added a bounded, unvalidated model example with measurement and failure conditions.
- Marked X-IB as a hypothetical scenario extension absent from the original public v1.

### Preserved
- `IBC_v1.md` remains unchanged as the historical public snapshot.

- Uncertainty, unexplained observations, negative results and competing interpretations remain part of the inquiry.

## v1 — Initial public working draft

### Added
- IBC as **Intelligent Beings: Competition and Coordination**
- competition and coordination as two interacting system forces
- system-definition requirement before evaluating conflict or benefit
- distinction between disagreement and core-interest conflict
- passive and proactive coordination
- survival pressure as a trigger for coordination
- dynamic system boundaries
- intelligence as a spectrum / “wavelength” metaphor
- distinction between intelligence and intelligent beings
- broader intelligent-being dimensions: intelligence, emotion, wisdom, capability, values, and residual \(R\)
- human, animal, cellular, and HMHI categories
- **HMHI — Human–Machine Hybrid Intelligence** as a provisional term for current AI
- task substitution vs. being replacement
- curiosity as a possible pre-necessity exploration mechanism
- positive attitude as an enhancer of creative coordination
- human values as important but reference-dependent
- business/economic and scientific R&D cases
- rights, authority, and moral-status questions
- “open lab for thinking” public-development model

### Public framing
- not a finished theory
- not a new governance system
- not an SOP
- not an ownership claim
- equations are thinking tools, not established natural laws

### Guiding principles
- Protect the question, not the proposal.
- Explore the process, not just the result.
