# Open Questions

IBC is intentionally incomplete. This file records questions that should remain open rather than be forced into premature answers.

## Intelligent beings

- What exactly defines an intelligent being?
- Is intelligence necessary but not sufficient for an intelligent being?
- What additional dimensions belong beside intelligence?
- How should emotion, wisdom, capability, values, embodiment, memory, and experience be represented?
- What remains inside the residual \(R\)?

## Intelligence spectrum

- Is a spectrum or “wavelength” metaphor useful enough to retain?
- What would a more rigorous mathematical representation look like?
- How should overlapping intelligence regions be represented?
- Can very different substrates express functionally similar intelligence?
- Which forms of intelligence have humans not yet learned to recognize?

## Human–Machine Hybrid Intelligence (HMHI)

- Is HMHI the best term for current AI systems?
- Which parts are genuinely hybrid and which are machine-specific?
- How should increasing machine autonomy change the term?
- At what point, if any, would “machine intelligence” become more accurate than HMHI?
- Do human and machine reasoning share common abstract principles while differing in implementation?

## Competition and coordination

- How do competition and coordination interact over time?
- When does competition become destructive?
- When does coordination suppress diversity?
- When does passive coordination become proactive?
- How does perceived survival threat change coordination?
- How do system boundaries shift under external threats?
- Can competition and coordination be modeled as emergent rather than centrally selected processes?

## System definition

- What is the minimum useful definition of a system?
- Which system boundary matters for a given conflict?
- How do nested systems alter interpretation?
- How often are apparent conflicts actually boundary errors?

## Curiosity

- Is curiosity partly independent of immediate survival needs?
- Which animals show open-ended curiosity rather than only instrumental exploration?
- Can HMHI develop curiosity-like behavior that is not directly reward-driven?
- Does curiosity create unusually low-friction coordination?

## Wisdom

- How should wisdom be distinguished from intelligence and capability?
- Which animals show wisdom-like behavior?
- Can wisdom be learned without lived biological experience?
- What would “machine wisdom” mean operationally?
- Can coordinated systems become wiser than their individual participants?

## Emotion

- Is emotion a separate dimension of an intelligent being?
- How should love, sympathy, attachment, trust, fear, and grief be represented?
- Can an intelligence recognize emotion without experiencing it?
- What would count as evidence of machine affect rather than simulation?

## Rights and authority

- Does intelligence have any relationship to moral status?
- How should animal interests be represented?
- Could HMHI ever acquire rights?
- Which properties would matter: intelligence, consciousness, agency, suffering, autonomy, continuity, or something else?
- Who should make final decisions when intelligent participants disagree?

## Economics and work

- Are companies overusing AI primarily for labor-cost reduction?
- What new products, services, and markets can human–HMHI coordination create?
- Can efficiency increase without reducing human labor participation?
- How should we distinguish local company optimization from whole-system optimization?

## R&D

- Which parts of scientific discovery benefit most from human–HMHI coordination?
- Which decisions should remain human-led?
- Which experimental bottlenecks can robotics reduce?
- How should uncertainty, negative results, and unexplained residuals be preserved?

## Larger questions

- What happens if one intelligent system becomes excessively dominant?
- Can diverse intelligent beings occupy complementary niches without replacing one another?
- What forms of collective capability appear only through coordination?
- How should human values be distinguished from universal structure?
- What becomes possible when different intelligent beings compete and coordinate within the same evolving world?
